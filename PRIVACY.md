# Privacy

TL;DR for Claude Code collects no data and sends nothing anywhere.

- **No network.** The mod makes no network calls and starts no processes.
- **What it reads.** The tool calls and results of your Claude Code session, and the files Claude edits in that session, to build the card and the net diff. It reads them on your machine only.
- **What it keeps.** Finished cards, at most 50 per session and without diff bodies, in Claude Code's local plugin store on your machine. Turn off the `keep` switch to keep nothing.
- **No accounts, no analytics, no third parties.**

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/kushalBanda/claude-tldr/issues.
